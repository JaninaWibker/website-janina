A list of restaurants I've come across and liked in various cities.

> 🌱 All have vegetarian and maybe vegan options, but most aren't exclusively vegetarian/vegan. 


### Karlsruhe

- [Fettschmelze](https://fettschmelze.org): Insanely good pizza
- [Rote Bete](https://www.rotebete-food.de): Syrian food, great for lunch and takeaway
- [Oxford Ost](https://www.oxford-cafe-ost.de) and [Oxford Pub](https://www.oxfordpub.de): Wide range of options including burgers, pasta, and salads. Popular with students and affordable
- [Dom](https://dom-grill.com)
- [Nahverzehr](https://www.vbk.info/service/nahverzehr-kantine-west.html): Very very good lunch option
- [Purino](https://purino.de/restaurant/karlsruhe-schloss-gottesaue/): Good pizza and pasta
- [My Heart Beats Vegan](https://www.myheartbeatsvegan.de): Vegan burgers and wraps
- [Yangda](https://www.yangda-karlsruhe.de)
- [Alina Café](https://alinacafe.de) and [Maschinenhaus](https://maschinenhaus-karlsruhe.de)

### Zürich

- [Kaisin](https://kaisin.ch)

- [Ach'i](https://www.achi.restaurant)
- [Miss Miu](https://www.miss-miu.ch/)

### Basel

- [Vito](https://www.vito.ch/de/standort/sbb-passerelle-basel): Incredibly good pizza as take-away inside the Basel SBB train station. Fast enough to get off the ICE, order pizza, and board it again before it leaves (tbf this isn't difficult to achieve as the ICE waits for about 15 minutes, but we did it in 6 minutes while ordering pizza for 4 people).
